# The assistant and confirmation

EquipmentExchange includes an assistant you can ask questions in plain language, such as "how do I block a machine for maintenance?" or "which of my listings are active?".

## Where its answers come from

The assistant answers from two sources:

- **These help documents.** They explain how the product works: rentals, quotes, listings, machines, yards and bookings.
- **Your own data.** For example, your listings, machines, yards, requests and bookings.

It does not answer from guesswork. If something is not covered by the help documents or your data, the assistant should say it does not know rather than invent an answer. Equipment specifications come from the equipment knowledge base, and a value that is not known is reported as not known.

## It always asks before changing anything

The assistant can help you make changes, such as preparing a quote or blocking dates on a machine. It never makes a change on its own.

Before changing anything, the assistant tells you exactly what it is about to do and asks you to confirm. Nothing changes until you say yes. If you say no, or do not answer, nothing changes.

For example, if you ask it to block a machine next week for maintenance, it will describe the block (the machine, the reason and the dates) and wait for your confirmation before creating it.

## Good habits

- **Read the summary before confirming.** Check the dates, amounts and machines it names.
- **Be specific.** Naming the listing, machine or dates gets a more precise answer.
- **Check important numbers.** Before sending a quote, review its line items and total yourself. A sent quote is locked and cannot be edited.

## What the assistant cannot do

The assistant works within the same rules as everything else in the product. It cannot edit a sent quote, cancel a booking that is already on rent, change a machine's specifications, or make anything visible to builders that is not published. The rules described in these documents apply to the assistant as well.
