# Glossary

Common terms used across EquipmentExchange.

**Builder.** A person or company renting equipment for a job.

**Supplier.** A person or company that owns equipment and rents it out.

**Listing.** What builders see: one equipment model offered by a supplier, with photos and a price per day. A listing is Draft, Active or Inactive. Only Active listings are visible to builders.

**Publishing.** Making a listing Active so builders can find it. A listing needs at least one active machine before it can be published.

**Machine.** One physical unit owned by a supplier. Each machine belongs to one listing and one yard, and has a model year, a condition and identifiers such as a serial number.

**Condition.** How worn a machine is: New, Excellent, Good, Fair or Poor.

**Retired machine.** A machine no longer in use. It is kept for history but is never available for rent.

**Fleet.** All of a supplier's machines.

**Yard.** A place where a supplier keeps machines. Each machine belongs to one yard.

**Block.** A date range when a machine is not available, for Maintenance, Transport or Held back. Blocks on one machine cannot overlap.

**Specifications.** Facts about a model, such as operating weight or engine power, taken from the equipment knowledge base. Unknown values are shown as not known.

**Rental request.** A builder's request to rent from a listing for a start and end date, with an optional message. It starts as `PENDING`; the supplier accepts or declines it.

**Quote.** The supplier's price for a request, made of line items, plus 18% GST.

**Line item.** One part of a quote: `RENTAL`, `TRANSPORT`, `OPERATOR`, `DEPOSIT` or `DISCOUNT`. Only `RENTAL` is required.

**Rate card.** A listing's standard prices. When one exists, the system can draft a quote automatically.

**Quote status.** `DRAFT` and `PENDING_APPROVAL` quotes can be edited. A `SENT` quote is locked.

**Booking.** The rental agreed when a builder accepts a sent quote. It starts as `CONFIRMED`.

**Booking status.** `CONFIRMED`, `OUT_FOR_DELIVERY`, `ON_RENT`, `RETURNED`, or `CANCELLED`. Cancelling is possible only while `CONFIRMED` or `OUT_FOR_DELIVERY`.

**GST.** Goods and Services Tax, charged at 18% on quotes.

**Amounts.** All amounts are in Indian rupees, with at most two decimal places.

**Assistant.** The built-in helper that answers from these documents and your data, and always asks for confirmation before changing anything.
