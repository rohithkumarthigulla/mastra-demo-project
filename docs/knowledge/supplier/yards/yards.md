# Yards

A yard is a place where you keep your machines, such as a depot, a storage site or a workshop. Recording your yards tells you, your team and the assistant where each machine is.

## Machines and yards

Each machine belongs to exactly one yard. When you add a machine, you choose its yard. If the machine moves to a different yard, update it so the record matches where the machine really is. See "Editing a machine".

A yard can hold many machines, and the machines in one yard can belong to different listings. For example, one yard might hold two excavators and a roller.

## Why yards matter

- **Planning delivery.** Knowing where a machine is helps you plan transport to the builder's site, and decide whether a `TRANSPORT` line belongs on your quote.
- **Choosing which machine to send.** When several machines on a listing are free, the one in the nearest yard is often the cheapest to deliver.
- **Keeping track.** If you have several sites, yards show you at a glance what is kept where.

## Setting up yards

Add your yards before you add machines, since every machine needs one. Describe each yard so you and your team can recognise it.

## Moving machines between yards

When you move a machine from one yard to another:

1. If the move takes the machine out of action for a few days, block those dates with the reason **Transport**. See "Blocking dates on a machine".
2. When the machine arrives, change its yard to the new one.
3. Lift the block if the move finished early, so the dates become available again.

## Yards and listings

Yards are not shown as part of a listing's model specifications. A listing describes an equipment model; yards describe where your individual machines are kept.

## Using the assistant

You can ask the assistant questions such as "Which machines are in my main yard?" or "Where is the roller with serial number ending 4471?" It answers from your own data. If you ask it to move a machine to another yard, it will describe the change and ask you to confirm before making it.
