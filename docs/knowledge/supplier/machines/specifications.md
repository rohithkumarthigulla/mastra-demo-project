# Machine specifications

Specifications are the facts about an equipment model, such as how heavy it is, how powerful its engine is, or how deep it can dig. Builders use them to decide whether a machine suits their job.

## Where specifications come from

Specifications come from the EquipmentExchange equipment knowledge base, not from you. When you create a listing, you choose a model from the knowledge base, and that model's specifications appear on the listing and apply to every machine on it.

This means every listing for the same model shows the same facts, whichever supplier offers it. Builders can compare like with like.

## You cannot change what a machine is

A supplier cannot edit specifications. You choose the model; the model decides the specifications. If you have a machine of a different model, it belongs on a listing for that model.

What you do record is information about your particular unit: its model year, its condition (New, Excellent, Good, Fair or Poor) and identifiers such as its serial number. See "Adding a machine".

## Examples of specifications

Which values apply depends on the type of equipment. Examples include:

- operating weight
- engine power and engine model
- maximum digging depth and maximum reach
- maximum lift height and lift capacity
- bucket capacity
- drum width, for rollers
- transport length

## Unknown values

Sometimes a value is not known for a model. In that case it is shown as not known. It is never guessed, estimated or filled in from a similar model.

If a builder asks about a value that shows as not known, answer from your own knowledge of your machine, for example by checking the manufacturer's plate or manual.

The assistant follows the same rule. If it is asked for a specification that is not known, it says the value is not known rather than guessing.

## Common questions

**A specification looks wrong. Can I fix it?** Not by editing your listing or machine. Specifications are shared across all suppliers for the model.

**Can I add an attachment's details?** Specifications describe the model. Mention attachments in your listing details and photos.
