# Your fleet of machines

Your fleet is every machine you own and rent out through EquipmentExchange. While a listing is what builders see, a machine is the physical unit you actually send to a site.

## Listings and machines

A listing presents one equipment model. The machines behind it are the individual units of that model you own. For example, a listing for a particular excavator model might have three machines: three separate excavators, each with its own serial number.

Each machine belongs to:

- **one listing**, which is the model builders see; and
- **one yard**, which is where the machine is kept.

A listing needs at least one active machine before it can be published.

## What is recorded for each machine

- **Model year.** The year the machine was made.
- **Condition.** One of New, Excellent, Good, Fair or Poor.
- **Identifiers.** Such as the serial number, so you can tell your machines apart.
- **Yard.** Where it is kept.

The machine's specifications, such as operating weight or engine power, come from the model in the equipment knowledge base, not from you.

## Active and retired machines

A machine you still rent out is active. When a machine leaves your fleet for good (sold, scrapped or too old to rent), retire it.

A retired machine:

- is kept for history, so past rentals still show which machine was used;
- is never available for rent again;
- does not count as the active machine a listing needs to be published.

Retire machines rather than deleting them, so your rental history stays complete.

## Availability

An active machine is available unless something makes it unavailable, such as a block you record for maintenance or transport. See "Machine availability" and "Blocking dates on a machine".
