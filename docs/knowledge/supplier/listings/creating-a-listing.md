# Creating a listing

A new listing starts as a Draft. Builders cannot see it until you publish it, so you can set it up at your own pace.

## Steps

### 1. Choose the model

Pick the equipment model from the equipment knowledge base: first the category (for example, excavator or wheel loader), then the specific model.

Choose carefully. The model decides which specifications appear on the listing, and you cannot edit those specifications yourself. They come from the knowledge base.

### 2. Add the details

Fill in the listing details: a title, a description, the city and location where the equipment is offered, and your price per day. Your price per day is the starting point when you prepare quotes for builders.

### 3. Add photos

Upload photos of your equipment:

- up to 10 photos;
- each up to 8 MB;
- JPEG, PNG or WebP only.

Use photos of the real equipment, taken in good light. Show the whole machine and any attachments that come with it.

### 4. Add machines

A listing describes a model. To rent it out, add the machines you actually own of that model. Each machine records its yard, model year, condition and identifiers such as a serial number. See "Adding a machine".

### 5. Publish

When the listing has at least one active machine, you can publish it. Publishing makes it Active and visible to builders. See "Publishing a listing".

## Saving a draft

You do not need to finish in one go. A Draft listing stays private until you publish it. Builders never see drafts and cannot send requests against them.

## Common questions

**Can I change the model later?** Yes, you can edit a listing's details, including the model. Remember that the specifications shown will change to match the new model, and every machine on the listing should be of that model.

**Can I enter my own specifications?** No. Specifications come from the equipment knowledge base so that every listing for the same model shows the same facts. A value that is not known is shown as not known.

**Why add photos?** Builders are far more likely to request equipment they can see. Clear photos of the real machine build trust.
