# Listings

A listing is what builders see when they look for equipment. It presents one equipment model that you rent out, with photos and a price per day. Builders send rental requests against a listing.

## What a listing contains

- **The model.** Chosen from the equipment knowledge base, for example a particular 20-tonne excavator. The model's specifications are shown on the listing automatically.
- **Photos.** Up to 10 photos of your equipment.
- **Details.** A title, a description, the city and location, and your price per day, which is the starting point for your quotes.
- **Machines.** The physical units you own of that model. Each machine belongs to exactly one listing.

A listing describes a type of machine. The machines attached to it are the units you actually send to site.

## Listing statuses

Every listing has one of three statuses:

| Status   | Visible to builders | Typical use                              |
| -------- | ------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Draft    | no                  | a new listing you are still setting up   |
| Active   | yes                 | a published listing builders can request |
| Inactive | no                  | a listing you have taken down for now    |

Only Active listings are visible to builders. Draft and Inactive listings are visible only to you.

## Before a listing can go live

A listing needs at least one active machine before it can be published. A listing with no machines, or only retired machines, cannot become Active, because there would be nothing for a builder to rent.

## Photos

- Up to 10 photos per listing.
- Each photo can be up to 8 MB.
- Accepted formats are JPEG, PNG and WebP.

Clear photos of the actual equipment help builders decide. A listing with good photos is easier to trust.

## Specifications

You do not type specifications into a listing. They come from the equipment knowledge base for the model you chose. You cannot change what a machine is. If a value is not known for a model, it is shown as not known rather than guessed.
